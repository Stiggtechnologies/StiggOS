# Public-facing copy

Approved language about how Stigg's operations are verified. Use these on the
marketing site, in proposals, and in client-portal welcome banners.

## Short (one sentence — for tooltips, footers)

> Stigg Security uses GPS-stamped checkpoint verification with photo evidence and supervisor review on every patrol.

## Standard (3 sentences — for proposals, About page)

> Stigg Security uses mobile patrol verification with GPS-stamped checkpoint scans, time-stamped reports, photos, and incident documentation reviewed by a supervisor. Company-issued patrol kits and key sets may also be equipped with asset trackers to reduce lost-key risk and support service accountability. Every action is logged to an immutable audit trail; clients see a curated view through their own portal.

## Detailed (paragraph — for the website's "How we work" section)

> Every Stigg patrol carries a phone running our field app. At each post, our officer scans a QR or NFC tag at the checkpoint — front door, mechanical room, parking lot, high-risk tenant area. The app records the scan with a GPS fix and timestamp. Anything abnormal triggers a photo and a structured incident report on the spot. For mobile patrols, a continuous GPS track confirms route coverage; missed-checkpoint windows automatically alert dispatch. Vehicle GPS, asset trackers on patrol kits and keys, and AI-monitored cameras all feed into the same operations console — your dedicated supervisor sees the same picture we do, and you see a curated version through your client portal.

## Compliance & privacy add-on

> Stigg is licensed under the Alberta Security Services and Investigators Act (SSIA). All personal data is handled per the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), with breach response procedures rehearsed quarterly and an audit log retained for the period required by law.

## Differentiation snippet (vs. competitors using paper or basic NFC apps)

> What makes our verification different: GPS plus QR plus photo plus supervisor — not just one of those. Coverage gaps surface within minutes, not at end-of-month. AI-camera events route through our own dispatch logic with rotating guard, monitoring, and law-enforcement escalation — independent of any single camera vendor.

## What NOT to claim

- Don't promise specific response-time SLAs in marketing copy. Those go in MSAs after a site survey.
- Don't claim "100% coverage" — claim "verified coverage with documented exceptions."
- Don't name specific clients without their written approval.
- Don't claim "AI prevents incidents." Claim "AI surfaces patterns and shortens response."
